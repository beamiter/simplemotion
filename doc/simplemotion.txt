*simplemotion.txt*  Visible-target motion for Vim9

:SimpleMotion          Type two characters and jump across visible windows.
:SimpleMotionDown      Jump to a visible line below the cursor.
:SimpleMotionUp        Jump to a visible line above the cursor.
:SimpleMotionHealth    Show configuration health.

Map the corresponding <Plug> targets to preferred keys.  Target labels come
from g:simplemotion_keys and are extended to multiple characters as needed.

vim:tw=78:ts=8:noet:ft=help:norl:
